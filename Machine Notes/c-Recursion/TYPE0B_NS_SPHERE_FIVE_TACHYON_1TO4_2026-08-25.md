### Möbius spin lift and the infinity BPZ phase

This is not a Ramond-sector effect and does not introduce a spin-structure
sum.  The intermediate representations remain NS.  It is a coordinate
convention required because the external picture-zero components are
holomorphic half-differentials.

For

\[
 f(z)=\frac{az+b}{cz+d},\qquad s^2=ad-bc,
\]

the five square roots of the local-coordinate derivatives must come from
one choice of \(s\).  For finite source and target the lift is
\(s/(cz+d)\).  When exactly one endpoint is at infinity the canonical local
coordinate supplies a factor of \(i\).  Independently taking five principal
square roots does not define a lift and gives spurious chart phases.

In addition, in the branch used by this block atlas a holomorphic
\(G_{-1/2}\) component in the comb's infinity slot carries the component
three-form phase \(+i\); the antiholomorphic phase is \(-i\).  This is a
local-coordinate/BPZ phase at the infinity slot.  It must not be confused
with multiplying every BRY odd pants coefficient by \(i\).  With these
factors, a general oriented channel and its reversed orientation agree at
fixed internal momenta to better than \(2\times10^{-12}\) in the automated
regression (typically \(10^{-15}\)).

There is a second, independent lift on each internal NS plumbing tube.  If
\(q=-r<0\), the holomorphic reduced block uses
\(\log q=\log r+i\pi\), whereas the antiholomorphic block must use the
conjugate logarithm \(\log r-i\pi\).  Calling the principal square-root
routine independently in both halves incorrectly assigns
\(+i\sqrt r\) twice.  This matters even when every external field is a
bottom NS primary: its exchanged NS module still contains
\(G_{-1/2}\) descendants and hence half-integer powers of \(q\).

The recursion API therefore accepts the lifted plumbing logarithms
explicitly, and the leading powers use the same logs.  At the pole-free
imaginary-energy control point with punctures
\((0,0.05,0.1,1,2)\), the two reassociations
\((0,1,2,3,4)\) and \((2,1,0,3,4)\) then agree at finite \(R=4\) cutoff to
relative residual \(1.88\times10^{-3}\) for five bottom primaries and
\(3.31\times10^{-3}\) for the component with descendants
\((1,1,1,0,0)\).  Before the conjugate-tube fix the corresponding residuals
were \(1.09\) and \(1.50\).  Thus the issue is an NS tube lift, not a
Ramond intermediate channel.

\[

\[

The split is therefore geometric and permutation-symmetric, while the
finite-part ledger is kept as separate analytic data.

For any of the \(5!=120\) oriented comb orderings \(\alpha\), let
\((q_{1,\alpha},q_{2,\alpha})\) be its linear-channel plumbing coordinates
and define

\[
 \rho_\alpha(x)=\max\bigl(|q_{1,\alpha}(x)|,
                           |q_{2,\alpha}(x)|\bigr).
\]

There are eight orientations of each of the fifteen labelled stable-tree
topologies.  All 120 are retained in the proposal atlas \(\mathcal A_c\).
A comb and its reversal have the same \(\rho\), so the geometric cells are
more precisely indexed by the 60 reversal classes \([\alpha]\); retaining
both representatives makes the proposal and its diagnostics manifestly
permutation-symmetric.  Away from ordinary equal-radius walls, moduli space
is split into

\[
 \mathcal V_{[\alpha]}=
 \left\{x:\rho_\alpha(x)<1,
 \quad \rho_\alpha(x)=\min_{\beta\in\mathcal A_c,\,\rho_\beta(x)<1}
 \rho_\beta(x)\right\}.
\]

At a sampled physical point \(x\), the code first minimizes \(\rho\) over all
120 charts.  If the selected representative places the incoming puncture in
the right cherry, it is reversed only afterwards to use the standard
endpoint-residue convention.  This leaves \(\rho\) and the geometric cell
unchanged.  The Liouville continuum, its path-ordered residue forest, and
every NS block are then evaluated by \(c\)-recursion in that channel.

The proposal chart is independent: if \(x\) was generated in chart
\(\gamma\), the moduli measure retains the \(\gamma\)-chart Jacobian, and the
importance weight is divided by the full 120-chart mixture density.
Consequently the adaptive evaluation channel neither changes the measure nor
double-counts overlaps.  At infinite recursion and quadrature order the cell
walls are merely changes of representation; at finite order minimizing
\(\rho\) suppresses truncation error.

Boundary subtraction is organized by the Deligne--Mumford strata themselves,
not by a recursion-method layer: ten stable divisors \(D_{ij}\) supply the
normal one-variable finite parts, and the fifteen compatible intersections
\(D_{ij}\cap D_{k\ell}\) supply the commuting bidisc finite parts.  In a
Voronoi chart these are simply \(q_1=0\), \(q_2=0\), and
\(q_1=q_2=0\).  The bulk, divisor, and corner pieces all use the same
five-point \(c\)-recursive block.  There is no \(h\)-recursive face layer.

If a boundary component remaining after the analytic finite part has radial
margin \(M_e>0\), its leading
density with respect to \(d^2q_e\) is

\[
 |q_e|^{-2+M_e}.
\]

Thus the channel-local disk proposal

\[
 p_e(q_e)=\frac{p_e}{2\pi}|q_e|^{-2+p_e}
\]

has finite variance for \(0<p_e<2M_e\), and the asymptotically matched
choice is \(p_e=M_e\).  The numerical driver stores both the audited
